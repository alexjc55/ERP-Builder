name: Marketing and sales boundary
description: User-stated departmental responsibilities for the proposed Marketing section.
---

The user wants Marketing to work through collected prospect contact lists and
occasionally existing clients, introduce the company and offer services for
their projects. Its positive outcome is a prospect willing to send project
information so the company can prepare a commercial quote.

Sales, not Marketing, owns requesting/collecting those documents, preparing
the quote and following up through quote signing.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined this division of responsibility on 2026-10-12.
This is a business requirement, not approval of any proposed UI or implementation.

**How to apply:** Preserve the handoff between departments. Do not make receipt
of documents or signed quotes prerequisites for Marketing to hand a lead to Sales.
